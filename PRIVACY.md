# Privacy Policy

_Last updated: August 29, 2026_

Armadillo is an independent tvOS client for [Pangolin](https://github.com/fosrl/pangolin). This policy explains what data Armadillo handles.

## Data collection

Armadillo does not collect, transmit, or sell any personal data to its developer or to any third party. Armadillo contains no analytics, advertising, or crash-reporting SDKs.

## Data Armadillo stores on your device

To connect to your Pangolin network, Armadillo stores the following locally on your Apple TV, in the system Keychain:

- Your authentication session token
- The address of the Pangolin server you connect to
- Your organization and site selection

This data never leaves your device except to communicate directly with the Pangolin server you configure — it is not sent to the developer or to any third party.

## Data sent to your Pangolin server

When you use Armadillo, your device communicates directly with the Pangolin server you choose to connect to (either your own self-hosted instance or Pangolin Cloud). That server is operated by you or by Fossorial, Inc. (for Pangolin Cloud), not by Armadillo's developer. Any data handling by that server is governed by its own operator's privacy practices, not by this policy.

## Network traffic

Once connected, Armadillo establishes an encrypted WireGuard tunnel between your Apple TV and your Pangolin server. Traffic routed through this tunnel is only visible to you and the server you connect to.

## Contact

Armadillo is an independent, community-built open source project. For questions about this policy, open an issue at [github.com/Kazuryy/armadillo-apple](https://github.com/Kazuryy/armadillo-apple/issues).
